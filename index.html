    Backup & import

    Copy saves everything: your songs, BPMs, rig numbers and every setlist. Paste a backup below and tap Load to restore it. You can also load a CSV exported from the Google Sheet; only Ready and WIP rows come in, and drum settings already entered are kept.

    Settings

    Everything saves on this device first, so the app works with no signal. When you're online it also syncs with your private GitHub repo, which keeps the iPad and your computer matched. Each device needs the access key once.
